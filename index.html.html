Rotation essentials

A rotation turns a shape around a fixed centre of rotation. The shape keeps the same size and shape. Every vertex turns through the same angle.

Direction and angle

  • 90° clockwise: quarter turn to the right
  • 90° anticlockwise: quarter turn to the left
  • 180°: half turn, either direction
  • 270° clockwise: same result as 90° anticlockwise

About the origin (0, 0)

90° CW: (x, y) → (y, −x)

90° ACW: (x, y) → (−y, x)

180°: (x, y) → (−x, −y)

Try it with your magnetic tiles

  1. Place one corner on a coordinate and identify all vertices.
  2. Mark a vertex A with a small sticker.
  3. Hold the centre of rotation fixed and turn the whole tile.
  4. Read the new position of A and the other vertices.

When the centre is not the origin, the shape travels around that centre rather than spinning in place.
